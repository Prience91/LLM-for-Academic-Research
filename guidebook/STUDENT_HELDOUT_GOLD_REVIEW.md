# Student held-out results: direct Codex gold review

Status: canonical policy extension, authorized by the operator on 2026-10-31.

## Scope and authority

Codex gold review is responsible for evaluating the scientific correctness and evidence
entailment of results generated by student models during held-out administration. This is a
post-generation evaluation stage, distinct from gold qualification of Qwen-authored benchmark
question/reference-answer pairs before benchmark freeze. The authoring rules alone did not
previously define this student-results stage; this extension establishes it explicitly.

Every administered case goes directly to Codex gold review, across every benchmark lane and
every compared variant, including base controls, fine-tuned models, merged models, and
quantized derivatives. Base controls receive the same review as the trained students.
GPT-OSS, a tested model, another local judge, or deterministic diagnostics must not replace,
screen, pre-admit, or supply the final scientific judgments for this stage. There is no OSS-first
or machine-reject-only route for student-result review. Reviewers receive no prior machine verdict.

The operator's 2026-10-31 instruction authorizes this direct-gold route for the existing five-run
comparison. Future review authorization must identify its exact frozen run/packet census;
documentation synchronization alone does not authorize a new evaluation or a new disclosure of
private review materials. Existing access and transfer controls still apply.

## Two distinct gold stages

1. **Benchmark-authoring gold:** inspect the teacher's question, evidence, obligations, and reference
   answer before admitting a case to a frozen benchmark. No student prediction is present.
2. **Student-results gold:** after administration, inspect the student's original sealed response
   against the frozen scientific rubric and evidence. Judge the response without rewriting it,
   rerunning the case, or changing benchmark admission decisions.

Completion of either stage does not stand in for completion of the other. Completed objective
diagnostics or completed generation must not be labelled completed scientific evaluation.

## Entry conditions and evidence isolation

- Complete administration, record failures, and seal original predictions and their audit traces
  before opening hidden references for student-result review.
- Bind the review census to run IDs, model/checkpoint revisions, original prediction hashes,
  question order, case IDs, benchmark/reference versions, rubric, index and retrieval bindings,
  configuration, exclusions, and exact review-packet hashes.
- Review the entire frozen census, including abstentions and failed requests. Preserve the declared
  denominator and any operator-authorized exclusion across all compared runs. Never select cases
  for gold review using answer quality, retrieval success, or a machine score.
- Keep hidden references, expected evidence IDs, and gold judgments outside student generation,
  query rewriting, retrieval restriction, and answer repair. Their use in the offline gold review
  does not authorize disclosure to a serving student or a public repository.
- Preserve the original released answer, raw citation-marked answer, exact retrieved passages and
  source identities, frozen reference evidence, required obligations, and runtime failure state.
  An unreleased repair draft is not the answer the user received; inspect it only as a separately
  labelled diagnostic.

## Required gold judgments

Record the following dimensions separately; do not infer one from another:

1. **Scientific correctness:** accuracy of substantive claims, including numbers and units,
   methods, populations, geography, timeframe, uncertainty, source ownership, and causal scope.
   Distinguish contradiction, unsupported factual assertion, and omission.
2. **Evidence entailment:** whether the passages actually cited by the student support the claims
   attributed to them. Topic similarity, valid marker syntax, retrieval of the expected paper, and
   agreement with a reference answer do not establish entailment. A correct claim with the wrong
   citation remains a citation-support failure. Distinguish absent retrieved support from a claim
   contradicted by the underlying scientific evidence.
3. **Required-point completeness:** map every frozen atomic requirement to exact answer anchors,
   marking it covered, partial, missing, or incorrect. Retrieval of evidence and the student's own
   coverage/planning assertions are not proof that the answer satisfies a requirement.
4. **Abstention and administration outcome:** distinguish a scientifically justified abstention,
   an avoidable failure to answer, a transport/runtime failure, and a response withheld by release
   validation. An abstention makes no positive scientific claim and must not receive a fabricated
   factual-accuracy or entailment score; it remains in task-completion accounting.

Review the full answer. Record exact claim/answer anchors, document/chunk or support-unit IDs,
brief source quotations where applicable, the reasoning for material findings, and unresolved
questions. A reference answer is a reviewed guide rather than an infallible substitute for source
evidence. Flag a reference/evidence conflict for operator adjudication; do not silently rewrite the
reference, redefine the rubric, or remove the case after seeing model results.

## Review records and unresolved outcomes

Each gold record identifies the reviewer as `codex_gold`, review-protocol revision, run/case ID,
prediction and packet hashes, per-axis judgments, obligation-level findings, claim/evidence anchors,
abstention status, and final review status. Separate scientific-error findings from review execution
failures and insufficient evidence.

Missing, malformed, hash-mismatched, or unavailable gold decisions remain unresolved and block a
claim of complete scientific evaluation. They must not be silently counted as passes or scientific
failures. A failed gold execution receives no automatic retry or substitute machine adjudication;
any further disposition requires explicit operator authorization with the original record retained.
This does not authorize regenerating, repairing, or selecting a better student prediction.

## Comparison and reporting

Freeze any numeric rubric, aggregation rule, and primary endpoint before gold scoring; a later
revision requires an explicit new protocol version and full, consistently applied review census.
Do not invent thresholds from observed model performance. Report per-axis judgments rather than
only a composite score. Keep answered-only results distinct from all-case task completion, and
report the exact denominator, abstentions, request failures, unresolved reviews, and exclusions.

Compare variants on identical case IDs, reference evidence, source/index bindings, and review
criteria. Blind model identity, parameter count, precision, previous objective scores, and prior
machine judgments where possible. Freeze and retain any anonymization/order mapping separately
from reviewer packets. Review each response on its own merits before unblinding comparisons.

For the operator-authorized comparison, evaluate the two 27B variants against each other and the
three 4B variants against each other. The existing scoped runs each have 77 eligible cases with
the deliberate paper-related exclusion retained: 385 run/case records in total. These scope
details do not satisfy or waive the canonical 100-case final-benchmark gate. Use paired case
comparisons; when reporting answered-only scores, also compare common-answered cases and disclose
selection effects. Do not claim quantized/BF16 equivalence from similar averages alone.

Gold findings are final evaluation outputs, not training feedback or permission to change prompts,
retrieval, thresholds, weights, checkpoints, or the benchmark. If findings inform such changes,
label subsequent reuse as diagnostic and use a newly sampled, sealed set for an unbiased final test.
Keep private answers, references, evidence, mappings, and review ledgers out of the public code
repository; publish the reusable protocol and appropriately authorized aggregate reports only.

## Implementation boundary

This document establishes review responsibility and the post-administration handoff. It does not
claim a new executable gold-review command or automatic semantic scorer exists. Existing scripts
that emit `scientific_reference_review = pending_separate_review` and
`scientific_accuracy_score = null` remain pending until the direct Codex gold census is complete.
Keep scientific results in a new, separately sealed review namespace; never overwrite the original
prediction seals or historical objective summaries to make them appear gold-reviewed.
